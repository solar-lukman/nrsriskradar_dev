# RiskRadar — Executive Summary

**Enterprise Risk Management Platform | ISO 31000 Aligned**

---

## The Problem

Organizations manage risk across disconnected spreadsheets, siloed tools, and manual processes — resulting in stale board reports, incomplete audit trails, compliance gaps, and no real-time visibility into enterprise risk posture. Board report preparation alone consumes 60–80 person-hours per quarter.

## The Solution

**RiskRadar** is a unified enterprise risk management platform that consolidates risk identification, assessment, mitigation, monitoring, business continuity, executive reporting, and ethics & compliance into a single, role-secured digital environment — purpose-built for ISO 31000 alignment.

## Platform at a Glance

| Module                   | What It Does                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Risk Register**        | Full lifecycle risk management with AI-powered scoring, mitigation tracking, and bulk operations |
| **Risk Matrix**          | Interactive 5×5 heatmaps with department filtering and inherent/residual views                   |
| **Business Continuity**  | BCP register with integrated Business Impact Analysis, recovery objectives, and test management  |
| **Executive Dashboards** | Real-time analytics, trend charts, and AI-assisted board report generation                       |
